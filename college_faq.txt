College Student Support FAQ

Q: What subjects are offered in Semester 3?
A: Semester 3 includes Data Structures, Object Oriented Programming,
Database Management Systems, Computer Organization,
Discrete Mathematics, and Web Technology.

Q: What is covered in Data Structures?
A: Data Structures covers arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues,
trees, and graphs.

Q: What is covered in Object Oriented Programming?
A: It covers classes, objects, constructors, inheritance,
polymorphism, and encapsulation.

Q: What is covered in Database Management Systems?
A: It covers SQL, tables, keys, relationships, and normalization.

Q: What is covered in Computer Organization?
A: It covers CPU, memory, registers, instruction sets,
cache memory, and pipelining.

Q: What is covered in Discrete Mathematics?
A: It covers logic, sets, relations, functions, graphs,
and mathematical reasoning.

Q: What is covered in Web Technology?
A: It covers HTML, CSS, JavaScript, HTTP, DOM,
and basic web concepts.

Q: Where can students get examination information?
A: Students should check official college announcements
and department notices for examination dates and instructions.

Q: Where can students get assignment information?
A: Students should check announcements from faculty members
and the department.

Q: What should a student do if they have a subject question?
A: Students can contact the concerned faculty member
or department.

Q: Is the information provided by this assistant official?
A: No. This project uses sample information for demonstration.
Students should verify important information with official
college sources.

Q: What can the AI Student Support Assistant help with?
A: It can answer questions about subjects, academic topics,
examinations, assignments, and basic student support information.
